# 🍓 Mochi & the Magic Minutes ✨

A cute, interactive countdown companion designed to make deadlines feel a little less stressful and a lot more fun! Meet Mochi — your tiny cheerleader who helps you stay focused, track milestones, and celebrate every little step towards your goal. ♡

## ✨ About the Project

Mochi is a personalized countdown companion that transforms an ordinary deadline into a fun and motivating experience. Instead of simply counting down the days, Mochi helps you break your goal into smaller milestones, stay on track, and receive gentle reminders along the way.

Designed with a cozy pastel aesthetic, playful sounds, and encouraging voice messages, Mochi is here to remind you that progress doesn't have to be perfect — it just has to keep going.

## 🌸 Features

- **Live Countdown:** Track days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining until your deadline.
- **Personalized Goals:** Customize your countdown for the deadline that matters to you.
- **Milestone Tracker:** Break your goal into smaller tasks and check them off as you complete them.
- **Progress Tracking:** Visualize your progress as you move closer to your finish line.
- **Gentle Reminders:** Set reminders to give yourself a little nudge when you need it.
- **Cute Sounds:** Choose from playful sound effects to make reminders more delightful.
- **Voice Motivation:** Receive spoken reminders with your remaining time, goal, and motivational quotes.
- **Customizable Experience:** Adjust voice options and reminder preferences.
- **Responsive Design:** Enjoy the experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **Local Storage:** Your countdown settings and milestone progress are saved in your browser.

## 🧸 The Idea Behind Mochi

Deadlines can feel overwhelming when everything seems like one big task. Mochi was created to make the journey towards a deadline feel more manageable.

By combining a countdown, smaller milestones, sound, and encouraging messages, Mochi turns the pressure of a deadline into a series of little victories.

Because sometimes, a tiny reminder saying *"You've got this!"* is all you need. ♡

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Set your goal and deadline.
4. Customize your milestones and reminder preferences.
5. Let Mochi cheer you on! 🍡
